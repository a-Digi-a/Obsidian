# Geomagnetism

The **earths magnetic field** can be viewed as a **bar magnet tiled at 11 degrees** 

However, there is a problem with this model. At temperatures **above 770 degrees celsius**, iron **ceases to be magnetic**. The **earths core** is far hotter than this. 

![[Pasted image 20260120110547.png]]

The earths magnetic field is actually caused by **electric currents** which are a result of the **motion of molten metals at the core**. This is called the **geodynamo effect**

At the surface of the planet the magnetic field ranges from $25-65\mu T$
