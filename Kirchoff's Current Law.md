# Kirchoff's Current Law


**Kirchoff's Current Law** states:

The **sum of currents** entering a **node** must **equal** the **sum of currents** leaving a **node**

for example:

![[Pasted image 20260403015039.png]]

Here $I_{1}$ and $I_{2}$ are **flowing in** to the **node**, while $I_{3}$ and $I_{4}$ are **flowing out** of the **node**. 

Therefore:

$$I_{1}+I_{2}=I_{3}+I_{4}$$
