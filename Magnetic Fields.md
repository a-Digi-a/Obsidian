# Magnetic Fields

## Sources of Magnetic Fields

There are **three main sources of magnetic fields**:

### Permanent Magnets
These are materials that are **already magnetic**, known as **ferromagnetic materials**
### The Earth
The **earth** has a huge **magnetic field**
### Current Carrying Wires
When you send a **current** down a long **wire**, it generates a **magnetic field** around the wire.
We can use the **right hand rule** to determine the direction of the magnetic field in a wire.
