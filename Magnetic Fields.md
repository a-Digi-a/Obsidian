# Magnetic Fields

## Sources of Magnetic Fields

There are **three main sources of magnetic fields**:

### Permanent Magnets
These are materials that are **already magnetic**, known as **ferromagnetic materials**
### The Earth
The **earth** has a huge **magnetic field**
### Current Carrying Wires
When you send a **current** down a long **wire**, it generates a **magnetic field** around the wire.
We can use the **right hand rule** to determine the direction of the magnetic field in a wire.

## Fields and Flux

A **magnetic field** is usually denoted by the letter *B*
**B = magnetic flux density**
**Unit = T (Tesla)**

*B* is also sometimes given the unit *G*, which stands for **Gauss** *(1000 T)*

## Magnetic Fields Around Wires

The **magnitude** of the **magnetic field** generated by current through an **infinitely long wire** is given by the equation:
$B=\frac{\mu_{0}l}{2\pi r}$
This is a form of [[Ampere's Law]]
