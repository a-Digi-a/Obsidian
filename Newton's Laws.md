# Newton's Laws

There are **three Newton's Laws**

## Newton's First Law 

A **body** remains at **rest**, or in **motion** at a **constant speed** in a **straight line**, unless it is **acted upon by a force**

A **body** in **motion**, stays in **motion**, unless acted upon by **another force**

## Newton's Second Law 

At any instant of time, the **net** **force** on a **body** is equal to the body's **acceleration** **multiplied** by its **mass** or, equivalently, the rate at which the body's **momentum** is **changing** with **time**

The **equation** given by this Law is:
- **F = ma**

**force** is **equal to** the **mass** **multiplied by** the **acceleration**

## Newton's Third Law 

If **two** **bodies** exert **forces** on each other, these **forces** have the **same** **magnitude** but **opposite** **directions**

For every **action**, there is an **equal**, but **opposite** reaction

## Where do Newton's Laws not Work?

- Very **small** particles - **quantum mechanics**
- Very **fast** particles - **special relativity**
- Very **massive** particles - **general relativity**
