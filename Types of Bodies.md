# Particle

## Definition

A body with **finite** mass but **insignificant size**
## Practically

A body whose **size is unimportant** for the **problem** at hand

## Examples

- Earth orbiting the sun
- Satellite orbiting the Earth
- Ball bearing fired from a BB gun

# Rigid

## Definition

A body of **finite size** that does **not deform**, even under the action of **large forces**

This does not actually exist

## Practically

Used in cases where the **deflection** is so small that it is **negligible**

**Mathematically** defined by "the **distance** between any **two** **points** on a rigid body is **constant**"

# Elastic
## Definition

Under the action of a **force F** that does not exceed the elastic limit the body will **deform and stop**

**Removing** the **force** allows the body to **return to its original shape**

## Examples

- Rubber (rubber bands)
- Steel (springs)
