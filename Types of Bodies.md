# Particle

## Definition

A body with **finite** mass but **insignificant size**
## Practically

A body whose **size is unimportant** for the **problem** at hand

## Examples

- Earth orbiting the sun
- Satellite orbiting the Earth
- Ball bearing fired from a BB gun
