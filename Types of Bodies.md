# Particle

## Definition

A body with **finite** mass but **insignificant size**
## Practically

A body whose **size is unimportant** for the **problem** at hand

## Examples

- Earth orbiting the sun
- Satellite orbiting the Earth
- Ball bearing fired from a BB gun

# Rigid

## Definition

A body of **finite size** that does **not deform**, even under the action of **large forces**

This does not actually exist

## Practically

Used in cases where the **deflection** is so small that it is **negligble**.
